Randomize heat environments

Experimental. Heat random dry rooms and cool the same number of random vanilla heated rooms, keeping overall heat coverage balanced.

ROM FX and the pause map are updated. Logic overlay is partial: heated rooms get the heated room flag in logic JSON, but heat frame costs on individual links and strats are not rebuilt. The generator probes for solvable seeds but results may still be imperfect.

When both water and heat are enabled, at most one balanced pair per axis is used to improve reliability.
