Randomize water environments

Experimental. Flood random dry rooms with water and dry the same number of random vanilla water rooms, keeping overall water coverage balanced.

ROM FX and the pause map are updated. Logic overlay is partial: newly flooded routes may require Gravity, but full water physics (jump height, walljump, speed booster strats, etc.) are not rebuilt. The generator probes for solvable seeds but results may still be imperfect.

Not used in Escape start mode. Disabled when “flood near spawn” testing flags are set via CLI.
