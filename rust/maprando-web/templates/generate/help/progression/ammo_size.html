Ammo Size Settings

This setting affects the size of resource expansions.

The default (Vanilla) setting for Missiles, Super Missiles, and Power Bombs is 5 per pack.

The default (Vanilla) setting for Energy and Reserve Tanks is 1 per pickup.

Regardless of these settings, a maximum limit is enforced in-game of the following maximum resource capacities:

  • Ammo expansions will not increase capacity above 999 missiles, 99 super missiles, or 99 power bombs. Ammo will still be replenished for the full amount up to these limits.
  • E-Tank expansions will not increase energy capacity above 1499 units (14 energy blocks). The effects of E-Tank energy refill under Quality-of-life options will still apply.
  • Reserve Tank expansion will not increase reserve capacity above 400 units. Since reserve tanks normally come empty, there is no benefit to collecting further Reserve Tanks.
