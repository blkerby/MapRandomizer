Pickup sizes

This setting affects the size of ammo and energy expansions.

The default is 5 Missiles, Supers, and Power Bombs per pickup and 1 E-Tank and Reserve Tank per pickup.

In all cases, the in-game ammo capacity will be capped at 999 Missiles, 99 Supers, and 99 Power Bombs, while energy and reserve capacity are capped at 1499 Energy and 400 Reserve Energy.
