Area locations

This setting controls the location of areas on the map:

  • Unrestricted: Areas are placed in random parts of the map.
  • Preferred: Areas have some tendency to be placed in a part of the map corresponding to the vanilla game.

Use the gear button to customize each area's preferred center location and the strength of this effect. Horizontal and vertical position rewards can be adjusted separately: 10 is the default for Preferred, and 0 means no preference along that direction. Higher values favor placing areas closer to their preferred centers.

Center X and Center Y give the preferred position in map tiles. Larger X values move the target to the right; larger Y values move it downward. These are preferred locations, not fixed positions. A center value is irrelevant when its position reward is 0.
