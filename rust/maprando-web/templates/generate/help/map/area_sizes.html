Area sizes

This setting controls how large each area is on average:

  • Unbiased: No area is favored to be larger or smaller than another. Individual maps can still have very different area sizes.
  • Familiar: Favors larger Brinstar, Norfair, and Maridia areas, a medium-sized Crateria, and smaller Wrecked Ship and Tourian areas.

Actual area sizes will vary. Landmark and heat/water settings also influence how rooms are divided among areas.

Use the gear button to adjust the preferred relative size of each area.
