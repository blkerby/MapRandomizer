Relabel completed areas

This setting changes which area labels are assigned to the generated areas of completed maps:

  • Off: Keeps the area names assigned during map generation.
  • Standard: Assigns Crateria to the area containing Landing Site and Tourian to the area containing Mother Brain. The remaining areas are named by size.
  • Size: Assigns area names from largest to smallest: Norfair, Brinstar, Maridia, Crateria, Tourian, Wrecked Ship.
  • Depth: Assigns area names from highest to lowest on the map: Crateria, Wrecked Ship, Tourian, Brinstar, Maridia, Norfair. Each area's highest map tile determines its position in this order.
  • Random: Assigns area names at random.

Relabeling can override the effects of the landmark, heat/water, area-location, and area-size settings. It changes area names without moving rooms.

Use the gear button to customize the ordering and whether the Ship and Mother Brain determine the names of their areas.
