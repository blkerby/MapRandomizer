Return paths

This setting controls the tendency for one-way paths to have a shorter or longer return loop:
  • Unrestricted: No preference for shorter return loops. After taking a one-way passage, the route back may be short or long.
  • Short loops: Favors shorter routes back after taking a one-way passage.

This is a tendency, not a guarantee. Use the gear button to adjust how strongly shorter return paths are favored: 1 is the default for Short loops, and 0 means no preference (Unrestricted). Higher values give a stronger preference for shorter return paths.

Details

On all settings, the randomizer guarantees that one-way passages always loop back around somehow, though the path back could be long and require certain items. This setting affects the typical length of those paths.

The "one-ways" considered here are situations where navigating in the reverse direction within a room may be impossible, even with all items collected, without techniques above Basic:

  • Crumble blocks in Spore Spawn Super Room, Green Pirates Shaft, Big Pink, and many other rooms.
  • Sand transitions.
  • Green gates, whether or not they are glitchable.
  • Speed blocks in Crateria Super Room and Crocomire Speedway.
  • The Super block in Big Pink's morph tunnel.
  • Acid in Amphitheatre.
  • The barriers in Spore Spawn's Room, Crocomire's Room, and Botwoon's Room.
  • Acid and the solid block in Acid Statue Room.
  • The Bowling Alley Chozo.
  • The walls separating the Main Street morph tunnel from the rest of the room (a "zero-way").
  • Mother Brain Room and Bowling Alley (top vs. rest) are also treated as zero-way.

Likewise, return paths are measured assuming all items have been collected, but without techniques above Basic.
