Heat and water tiers

Rooms are tagged with tiers 1–3 in map-gen's room definitions. Each number is the preferred probability for placing that tier's rooms in Maridia (water) or Norfair (heat).

Probabilities must be positive, nondecreasing across the tiers, below 1, and at least the corresponding area's share of the size weights. Unrestricted tracks that share automatically; editing a number creates an explicit preference.
