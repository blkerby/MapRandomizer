Landmarks

Force selected landmarks (Ship and major bosses) into their original areas:

  • None: No landmarks are forced into their original areas; they can still be placed there by chance.
  • Ship + Mother Brain: Forces Landing Site into Crateria and Mother Brain into Tourian.
  • All: Forces all six landmarks into their original areas.

Use the gear to customize exactly which landmarks are forced into their original areas. Landmarks set to Off have no preference for their original areas.

The area constraints here may be overridden by "Relabeling completed areas" settings.
