Heat and water

This setting affects the extent to which heat/water rooms tend to be placed in Norfair/Maridia respectively.

  • Unrestricted: Adds no preference for placing heated rooms in Norfair or water rooms in Maridia beyond the selected area-size proportions.
  • Concentrated: Favors placing heated rooms in Norfair and water rooms in Maridia.

Even when "Concentrated" is enabled, heat and water rooms are still expected to occur in other areas.

Details

Use the gear button to adjust separate settings for heat and water, each with placement preferences for three groups of rooms called tiers. Tier 1 has the weakest preference for its original area, tier 2 has a stronger preference, and tier 3 has the strongest preference. Note that the Ridley and Draygon boss rooms are excluded from these settings, as they are covered by the "Landmarks" settings.

Each tier's number is the preferred chance of placing its rooms in Maridia (water) or Norfair (heat). For example, 0.5 means 50%. This is a target across generated maps, not a guaranteed fraction of rooms on each map.

  • Values must be greater than 0 and less than 1.
  • Each tier's value must be at least as high as the previous tier's value.
  • Values must also be at least the area's share of the total area-size weights. For example, if Maridia has one quarter of the total weight, its water values must be at least 0.25.

Unrestricted automatically follows that area's share when you change area sizes. Entering a number switches to a custom preference.
